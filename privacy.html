Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 4, 2026 · Last updated: May 4, 2026

GameWeave is operated by Boris Rayskiy ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the GameWeave Android app and its server collect, how that information is used, and the choices you have about it.

1. Who can use GameWeave

GameWeave is intended for users 13 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has registered an account, contact us at the address below and we will delete the account.

2. Information we collect

We collect only the information we need to operate the service.

2.1 Information you give us

  • Email address. Required to create an account, sign in, and reset a forgotten password. Stored on our server as the unique account identifier.
  • Password. Stored only as a one-way bcrypt hash. We never store, log, or transmit your plaintext password after the moment you submit it for sign-in or sign-up.
  • Display name. A name shown to other players at a game table. Defaults to a derivative of your email; you can change it at any time from the Profile screen.
  • Avatar image. Optional. If you upload an avatar, the image is stored in Google Cloud Storage; the URL pointing to it is associated with your account. You can remove or replace your avatar at any time from the Profile screen.

2.2 Information generated by play

  • Per-game statistics. For each finished game, we store the game ID, table ID, outcome (won / lost / drawn), and timestamp. These power the stats summary on your Profile screen and may be used in aggregate to improve the service.
  • Game state during a session. While you are connected to a table, the server holds the in-memory game state (your hand, bids, scores) so it can route turns and broadcast updates. This state is not persisted after the table ends; only the per-game outcome (above) is saved.

2.3 Information collected automatically

  • Install identifier. When you first launch the app, GameWeave generates a random identifier and stores it locally on the device. This identifier is sent with each diagnostic event to help us correlate events from the same install. It is not linked to your phone's advertising ID, IMEI, or any device fingerprint.
  • Diagnostic events. The app emits a small number of events — application launches, WebSocket connect / disconnect, errors — to help us understand stability and performance. Events include the install identifier, app version, build variant, and event-specific fields (e.g., the error message for a connect failure). They do not include the contents of any game state, your password, or your messages.
  • Network metadata. Our server logs include your IP address, request path, and timing for the duration retained by Google Cloud Run's standard logging (~30 days).

3. How we use your information

  • Operate the service. Authenticate sign-ins, route real-time game traffic, render the lobby and your profile, deliver password-reset emails.
  • Improve the service. Diagnose crashes, measure connection reliability, understand which games are played and how often.
  • Communicate with you. Send password-reset emails when you request them. We do not send marketing email.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not show ads in the app.

4. How we share your information

We share information only with the third parties listed below, and only as needed to operate the service:

  • Google Cloud Platform — hosts our server, database, and avatar image storage. Subject to Google Cloud's privacy and security commitments.
  • Google Play / Google Play Billing — handles in-app purchases of chips, when the chip-purchase feature ships. Google receives the purchase token and the price; we receive a confirmation that the purchase succeeded. Google Play's own privacy policy governs that data.
  • Email delivery provider. When you request a password reset, the email is sent through a transactional email service. The service receives your email address and the reset email body.

We will disclose information if required by law (subpoena, court order, or other legal process), and we may disclose information to protect our rights or the safety of our users.

5. How long we keep your information

  • Account data (email, password hash, display name, avatar URL) — retained until you request account deletion.
  • Per-game statistics — retained until you request account deletion.
  • Diagnostic events — retained for up to 90 days, then deleted.
  • Server logs — retained per Google Cloud Run defaults (~30 days), then deleted.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Edit your profile. You can change your display name and avatar at any time from the Profile screen.
  • Delete your account. Email us at the address below and we will delete your account, profile, avatar image, and per-game statistics within 30 days. We may retain hashed transaction records longer if required by tax or fraud laws.
  • Export your data. Email us and we will send you a JSON export of the data tied to your account within 30 days.
  • Sign-out. You can sign out of the app at any time from the menu. Sign-out invalidates the refresh token; subsequent sessions require a fresh sign-in.

If you are in the European Union, the United Kingdom, or California, you have additional rights under the GDPR / UK GDPR / CCPA, including the right to access, correct, delete, and port your data, and the right to object to processing. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

7. Security

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt before storage. All traffic between the app and our server is encrypted in transit (HTTPS for HTTP requests, WSS for WebSocket connections). Avatar uploads are scanned for valid image content before being stored.

No system is perfectly secure. If we discover a breach affecting your data we will notify you within the timeframes required by applicable law.

8. International transfers

Our server runs in us-central1 (Iowa, USA). If you access the app from outside the United States, your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top and, for material changes, also notify you within the app or by email. Continued use after the change means you accept the updated policy.
